About

The Company

Infinitum Financial is a boutique business and financial solutions firm. We partner with founders, operators, and finance leaders who need senior-level expertise without the overhead of a full-time hire.

Founded in 2017, we've built our practice on the premise that small and mid-sized businesses deserve the same caliber of strategic finance, valuation, and operational guidance that large enterprises take for granted. Our clients span manufacturing, professional services, technology, real estate, and nonprofit sectors.

What sets us apart is range. The same team that builds a discounted cash flow model can sit across the table from a lender, draft a working capital strategy, automate a recurring journal entry, or testify to the integrity of its methodology in a courtroom. That breadth lets us solve problems holistically rather than handing clients off between specialists.

We work on engagements as short as a single valuation report and as long as multi-year embedded finance leadership. Every client relationship begins the same way — with a conversation about what you're trying to build and where you're getting stuck.
