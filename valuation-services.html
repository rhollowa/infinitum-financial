Valuation Services

Business Valuation

Knowing the value of your business, your competitor's, or a new initiative under consideration is imperative. This information is a must for increasing the value you wish to add or extract. The corporate actions that offer you this opportunity come in many forms:

  • Raising equity capital
  • Preparing for a merger or acquisition (M&A)
  • Family law mediation and litigation
  • Dissolving a partnership
  • Expert witness testimony
  • Stock buyback and equity repurchase
  • Considering a spin-off of an element of your operations
  • Quantifying costs and benefits of corporate actions
  • Second opinion on a previous valuation
  • Debt covenant compliance
  • Presentations to the Board of Directors
  • Project IRR and NPV analyses for strategic decision-making

Whatever your reason, we're here to help. Our team produces reports and analyses that are accurate, defensible, and compelling. In addition to presenting the story the numbers tell, we provide thorough scenario analyses to inform decision-making and strategy. Knowing that the information we prepare for you is a means to an end, our team empowers you with information about the best course of action and provides guidance on what to expect from those decisions.

Our valuation methodologies apply to businesses of all sizes and industries. We approach every project with an unbiased, empirical method that considers all aspects of a project, from a business's core fundamentals to the most granular financial details that could impact the bottom line. The numbers don't lie, but how a stakeholder understands the story they tell could have a profound impact on the profitability of their enterprise. We take our role in communicating valuable business insights to you, our client, very seriously.

Asset Valuation

Seeing the Challenge, Embracing the Opportunity

Determining the value of financial assets can be intimidating. Poor record-keeping, inaccurate source data, and labyrinthine financial statements are just a few of the hurdles to assigning a value to your assets. Other firm-specific challenges of note might include creating a plan to fund larger-than-anticipated pension obligations in a bear market, forecasting liquidity to increase the performance of under-utilized cash, and calculating the capital structure that works best for your company's bottom line.

Working Smarter, Not Harder

Through a combination of forensic accounting, market research, and quantitative analysis, we provide results that we stand by. Our team is well-equipped to present our findings in a way that is coherent, comprehensive, and compelling to all audiences.
