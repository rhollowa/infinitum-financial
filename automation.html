Services

Automation

Infinitum was founded on the need to provide smarter solutions to complex problems. Long before "AI" became a buzzword, our team was building custom automation to eliminate the busywork that bogs down finance and accounting teams. That practice is still at the core of what we do.

What we automate

Take advantage of the service that has been in demand since day one.

Simplify decision-making with scenario analyses and statistical modeling

Build models that let you test assumptions, stress-test forecasts, and see the full range of outcomes — not just the base case. Monte Carlo simulations, sensitivity tables, and what-if dashboards that update the moment your inputs change.

Streamline data collection, analysis, and forecasting

Stop pulling numbers from five different systems by hand. We build pipelines that gather data from your accounting software, banks, vendors, and the web — clean it, structure it, and deliver it to the spreadsheet or dashboard where you actually work.

Create and deliver reports to your stakeholders, internal and external

Monthly board packets, investor updates, client statements, lender covenant reports. Whatever you send on a recurring basis, we can build a system that generates it, formats it, and emails it on schedule — or on demand.

Consider these tasks performing themselves

Automation pays for itself when the time you reclaim outpaces the cost of building it. Most of our automation engagements break even inside a quarter and compound from there. The question isn't whether to automate — it's which task to start with.
