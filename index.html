Your Needs, Our Strengths

Solutions Built Around You

Valuation

Whether you're divesting your share in a partnership or negotiating the sale of your business, assessing the value of your estate for legacy planning, or navigating a divorce and need to know what you may be entitled to — we're here for you. Our team will help you value what you've spent years building.

Risk Management

Risk is unavoidable, so managing risk is a must for protecting your assets. Whether it's a hedging program against interest rate and currency fluctuations, or SOX compliance and stress testing, we have the expertise to insulate you from unforeseen losses across the spectrum. Our team offers bespoke risk management programs specifically tailored to the unique needs of your business.

Automation

Eliminate the wasted hours taken up by performing mundane tasks by hand. Update balances and ledger entries, email client statements, and pull data from the web on demand or as scheduled. Our team will help you integrate our customized solutions with the platforms you already use to reduce process time, freeing you to focus on the more pressing tasks at hand.

Accounting Support

Mapping journal entries is not why you started your business. Don't get bogged down in the minutiae of bookkeeping and general ledger administration. We understand that prioritizing accurate record-keeping can be laborious and complex — with our white-glove, fully customized accounting solutions, you can feel safe knowing our team has you covered from implementation all the way through to quality control.

Corporate Finance

Leadership in your financial vertical can be costly if handled in-house. Our experienced team is here to protect the integrity of your financial statements and your bottom line. Accounting, FP&A, budgeting, variance analysis — name it. We're here to serve the underserved aspects of your financial operations.

Investment Strategy

Assumptions about defined benefit obligations made in the past may no longer hold true in today's economy. Changes in life expectancy, investment protocols, and market returns can create unexpected cash needs for your organization. Insulating the bottom line from unfunded pension obligations may require rethinking your investment policy statement, recommending a new allocation strategy, or reevaluating fund manager performance. We're here for you, and ready to stop at nothing to find a strategy that works for you for the long haul.
